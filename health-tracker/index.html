    How these were worked out

    Calories use the Mifflin–St Jeor equation times your activity level. Protein is set per kg of body weight for your goal, fat at about 27% of calories, and carbs fill the rest. Water is 35 ml per kg, plus 500 ml on workout days. These are estimates: weigh yourself weekly and adjust calories by 100–200 kcal if your weight isn't moving the way you want after 2–3 weeks.

    Your data
